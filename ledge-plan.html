Personal Mac app · build plan · v4

Ledge, an Alcove-style notch for your MacBook Pro

It uses the same look as Alcove: a small black island that grows out of the notch to show your music, volume and charging, then melts back in. It has fewer features and is built only for you.

Who builds it? AI does all of it. I write the code, build it, test it and install it, and you try each version and tell me how it feels. We match Alcove's look by designing it ourselves, so nothing is copied from their app, and since it's never released, that's fine for personal use.

How it will look

Five island states and a clean menu bar

Pure black, rounded, and only a little wider than the notch until you open it. Colors in the player come from the album art. The song name is an example.

What's in, what's out

A smaller app that does a few things well

Building

  • The Alcove-style island with smooth, springy animations
  • Now playing: compact view and full player
  • Swipe to skip tracks

  • Start at login, hide in full screen
  • A clean menu bar: only Wi-Fi, battery, Control Center and the date and time on the right

What we'll build, in order

Five milestones

Each one ends with a version you can install and use. You check it before I start the next.

1Island

The island itself

  • A black island that sits exactly on your notch
  • Opens on hover or click with a springy animation, and closes when you move away
  • Short hover delay so it doesn't open when you just reach for the menu bar
  • Hides in full-screen apps and only appears on the screen with the notch

You check: does it feel like Alcove when it opens and closes?

2Music

Now playing

  • Compact view with album art and a colored waveform whenever something plays
  • Full player with title, artist, scrub bar and controls
  • Swipe on the island to skip or go back
  • Works with Apple Music, Spotify and browsers, meaning anything that shows in Control Center

You check: play music from the apps you use and see whether it always shows up.

3Indicators

Volume, brightness, charging

  • Slim level bar when you press the volume or brightness keys
  • Charging pop when you plug in, and a low-battery warning

You check: press the keys and plug in the charger.

4Finish

Make it a real app

  • Installed in Applications with its own icon, starts at login, no Dock icon
  • Small settings window (on/off for each feature)
  • Battery and CPU check so it stays light
  • One-command update script

You check: use it for a week, and anything that bugs you goes on the fix list.

5Menu bar

Clean up the top right

  • Turn off Apple's extra icons (Bluetooth, sound, Focus, Spotlight, Siri and so on) using the Menu Bar page in System Settings. I'll give you the exact switches.
  • No third-party icons on the right, ever. Ledge hides every other app's icon, and the apps keep running normally in the background
  • Apps you install later get hidden automatically, so you never have to clean up again
  • If you ever need one of those apps' menus, a keyboard shortcut shows the hidden icons for a few seconds and then hides them again
  • Bluetooth, sound and Focus are still one click away in Control Center
  • Right side ends up as: Wi-Fi, battery, Control Center, date and time, and nothing else

You check: is the right side clean, and does the shortcut bring icons back when you need them?

Things to know up front

Limits and risks

Medium

Music info relies on a hidden macOS feature. Apple has locked it down in recent versions. We'll use the workaround other notch apps use, with Apple Music and Spotify as a backup. A future macOS update could break it, and then I'd update the code.

Medium

The normal volume pop-up may still appear. macOS doesn't let apps reliably hide its own volume and brightness display, so both might show for a moment.

Medium

The Control Center icon can't be removed. macOS doesn't allow hiding it, so the top right will be Wi-Fi, battery, Control Center and the date and time. That was true as of macOS 26, and I'll check whether 27 changed it. Hiding other apps' icons can also break after macOS updates, and I'd fix it when it does.

Low

Privacy dots still appear. The small orange, green or purple dot that macOS shows while your microphone, camera or screen recording is in use can't be hidden by any app. It only shows while something is recording.

Low

Matching the feel takes tuning. I can build the look and take screenshots, but I can't feel the animation. Expect a round or two of "faster, bouncier" from you.

Low

The project needs a permanent home. This chat uses a temporary folder. Before coding starts, we'll move to a folder you pick, such as Developer/Ledge.
